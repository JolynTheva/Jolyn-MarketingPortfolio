Who I am

A bit about me.

Hi, I'm Jolyn. I'm a fourth-year Business Administration student at the Lazaridis School of Business and Economics, Wilfrid Laurier University, concentrating in Marketing with a minor in User Experience (UX) Design.

My experience spans government, nonprofit, and student leadership: I've worked as a Marketing and Communications Assistant with the Government of Ontario's Domestic Marketing unit (Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Agribusiness), as a marketing intern with Super Athletes Ontario, a nonprofit supporting underprivileged families through sports summer camps, and as Co-President of Human Resources Laurier and a Campus Ambassador at Laurier's Waterloo campus. Across all of these, I'm drawn to the intersection of strategy and creative execution, figuring out not just what a brand should say, but how it should be perceived by its targeted audience and stakeholders.

Outside of school and work, I'm usually planning my next hike or trip, trying out a new sport, or hunting down a new cafe to try out.

Selected work

Projects.

Super Athletes Ontario

Growth Strategy

Partnered with SAO, a youth sports camp organization serving kids ages 5–13 across Ontario, on a full brand and digital overhaul across two summers, rebuilding their website from the ground up and leading content strategy for their signature annual event, Fuel the Fun. I led a complete website reconstruction on Framer, replacing SAO's previous site entirely, and designed a full suite of Instagram content in Canva for Fuel the Fun, including a pinned banner, a Save the Date post, and a multi-slide FAQ carousel.

Impact

Signups for the following summer tripled, and the organization gained increased recognition and support from municipalities and towns.

Foodland Ontario

Styling the Story

Contributed directly to Foodland Ontario's content and campaign work, spanning both creative planning for photoshoots and final social media design. I conceived and styled food photography concepts, including the soccer-themed charcuterie and brie boards created for the 2026 FIFA-related campaign tying into the Government of Ontario's sponsorship, and contributed to the planning, coordination, and strategy of photoshoots, including building mood boards to communicate visual direction to photographers. I also designed final graphics in Canva for Instagram posts spanning recipe features, seasonal content, and sponsorship campaigns.

Impact

Instagram engagement nearly tripled over the four-month period compared to the previous quarter.

Human Resources Laurier

Building the Brand, Season After Season

Contributed to Human Resources Laurier across three years, taking on increasing ownership of the club's marketing, operations, and internal communications. I led external marketing efforts including Instagram content for HRL's signature events, most notably HRPA Night, and designed and managed a full slide deck system for biweekly VP meetings that kept a multi-portfolio executive team aligned and on schedule. I also conceived the HRPA Night giveaway strategy to drive engagement, contributing to both internal and external marketing initiatives throughout my time with the club.

Impact

HRPA Night promotional content drove turnout that exceeded expectations, and the club's social media engagement has nearly doubled since 2023.

Canadian Produce Marketing Association

CPMA Booth Experience

As part of the same Foodland Ontario co-op term, managed Foodland Ontario's presence at the CPMA trade show, handling both the logistics and the visual and experiential design of the booth. I managed booth staffing schedules and coordinated event materials, planned the booth's layout and flow, including its physical orientation and visibility to passing attendees, and developed talking points for staff along with handouts for visitors, while also directing the visual styling of the space.

Impact

The booth saw high engagement from attendees and opened new opportunities for future partnerships and potential clients.
